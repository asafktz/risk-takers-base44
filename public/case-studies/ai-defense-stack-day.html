04 / Format

Designed for a useful conversation

The show gave companies a focused presentation, then put their ideas under live panel questioning. The questions were specific enough to be useful in later follow-up.

On stage

Five focused company showcases

Each presenter had a dedicated segment and a separate panel discussion, giving viewers a clear story and a chance to hear the hard questions.

In discussion

Real implementation questions

The panels pressed on identity integration, cloud security decisions, continuous testing, and how teams evaluate AI-based security approaches.

After the show

Evidence a sales team can use

Participating teams received ranked registration lists, source-aware enrichment, connection records, panel Q&A summaries, and 11 replay choices: the full show plus five presentations and five panel cuts.
